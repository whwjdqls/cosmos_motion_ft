These are the two sep-2 50/50 V2M/M2V runs. They are distinct from the concurrent sep-1 image-joint baseline and camera-prediction comparison on the same page. Both sep-2 models omit camera prediction. Their controlled arm difference is bridge attention direction; do not label either as the sep-1 camera-prediction model.

Both exact **50k checkpoints** are evaluated on the same **71 held-out windows** (one per recording), with **284 predictions and zero skipped cases**. Checkpoint pinning protects these exact weights from training retention.

Both models use the same Phase1 video-projection EMA200k and selected Phase2 200k specialists, 50/50 V2M/M2V training, clean-motion E_M(0), batch32 and no camera-prediction loss. Their controlled training difference is bridge attention direction.

Inference: native UniPC35, motion shift3, guidance1; T97/20fps, fixed original case seeds and supplied neutral body shape. Video-only uses empty text. Video+text uses the full saved caption in both frozen reasoners. Caption conditioning is an inference intervention; Phase3 V2M training was caption-free. There is no camera-trajectory input, target-motion input or generated grounding/reset.
