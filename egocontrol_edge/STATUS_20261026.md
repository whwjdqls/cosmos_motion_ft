## Fairness decisions

The matched conditions and remaining differences are specified in README.md.
The primary benchmark gives both methods the first RGB frame and the same
clean SOMA motion, without semantic captions or extra camera trajectories.
Use the same held-out cases, seeds, UniPC35/shift10/CFG1, RGB preprocessing and
generated-frame-only PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS; keep GT visible in comparisons.

Report parameters and M2V exposure as well as update count. This baseline has
815458240 new pose parameters plus6422528 inherited trainable LoRA parameters.
Ours has Phase2 pretraining and historically used captions during M2V training;
the representation also differs (EgoControl Body23 versus UniEgo30). Preserve
baseline100k and200k:100k gives the same approximate M2V exposure as original
50/50 Phase3 at200k;200k supports the equal-update comparison. Do not use test
results for selecting hyperparameters or claiming a pure architecture effect.
